LinguaPath ("we") is run by [Company name], [address]. Contact: [privacy@example.com].

What we collect

  • Account: name, email, password (stored only as a salted hash), optional display name, bio, photo, native language, time zone, app language and, if you give it, your date of birth.
  • Learning: your courses, plan, lecture progress, quiz and exam answers, marks, certificates, saved words and notes.
  • Practice: what you type or say in practice. Voice recordings for practice are deleted after 30 days; recordings in monthly exams are kept with the marked exam.
  • Social: friends, chat messages and group membership, reports you make or receive and moderation decisions.
  • Device: a device id, platform, app version and a push token so we can send reminders. Crash reports contain the error, app version and device type.

Why we use it

  • To run your course, mark your work and issue certificates (contract).
  • To send reminders you have switched on (consent — change them any time in Profile → Edit profile).
  • To keep chat safe: messages are scanned automatically for abuse and may be read by a moderator when reported (legitimate interest).
  • To fix bugs and keep the service secure (legitimate interest).

Service providers

Text you send to the AI tutor, translator or pronunciation checker is processed by our providers ([DeepSeek], [Microsoft Azure AI services]) only to answer you; we do not send your name or email with it. Push notifications go through Google Firebase and Apple. Email goes through [email provider]. Data is stored in [region].

How long we keep it

  • Practice voice recordings: 30 days. Read notifications: 90 days.
  • Deleted accounts are anonymised immediately; remaining practice text, translation history, notifications and devices are removed within 30 days.
  • Certificates stay verifiable by their number unless revoked.

Your rights

In the app you can download your data (Profile → Download my data), correct your profile and delete your account. You can also write to us to object, restrict processing or complain to your data protection authority.

Children

LinguaPath is for learners aged 13 and over. Learners under 18 can chat and compete only with people they have accepted as friends.
