Delete your LinguaPath account

In the app: Profile → Account → Delete account. Confirm with your password (or type DELETE if you signed in with Google or Apple).

What happens

  • At once: your name, email, photo, bio, date of birth and sign-ins are removed and you are signed out everywhere. Other learners see "Deleted user".
  • Within 30 days: practice recordings and text, translation history, notifications and device records are deleted.
  • Kept without your identity: anonymous course statistics, and chat messages already delivered to others (shown as from "Deleted user").
  • Issued certificates stay verifiable by their number so employers can still check them; ask us if you want one revoked.

Can't open the app?

Email [support@example.com] from the address on your account and we will delete it within 30 days.
